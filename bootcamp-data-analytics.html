Comprehensive Syllabus

What You Will Learn

80 hours of live interactive sessions and hands-on laboratory exercises divided into 5 structured pillars.

Pillar 01

Advanced Excel & Data Wrangling

Build rock-solid foundations in spreadsheet analytics. Master lookup functions, logical conditions, data hygiene, dynamic pivot tables, and interactive executive reporting dashboards.

  • XLOOKUP, INDEX/MATCH, Dynamic Arrays
  • Data Cleaning & Text Transformation
  • Pivot Tables, Calculated Fields & Slicers
  • Interactive Financial & Sales Dashboards
Pillar 02

Python for Data Analysis

Learn Python programming from scratch with focus on analytics workflows. Automate data tasks, manipulate large tabular datasets with Pandas, and construct statistical charts.

  • Python Core & Data Structures
  • NumPy Arrays & Mathematical Operations
  • Pandas DataFrames, GroupBy & Merges
  • Visualizations with Matplotlib & Seaborn
Pillar 03

Relational Databases & SQL Mastery

SQL is the universal language of data. Query relational databases, design efficient schemas, execute complex multi-table joins, and write analytical window functions.

  • SELECT, WHERE, GROUP BY & HAVING
  • INNER, LEFT, RIGHT & FULL OUTER JOINs
  • Subqueries & Common Table Expressions (CTEs)
  • Window Functions (ROW_NUMBER, RANK, LEAD, LAG)
Pillar 04

Tableau & Business Intelligence

Turn raw metrics into visual stories. Connect to databases, design clean KPI cards, create calculated fields, build interactive filters, and publish dashboards for decision-makers.

  • Data Connections & Relationship Modeling
  • Calculated Fields & Level of Detail (LOD) Expressions
  • Interactive Dashboard Actions & Parameters
  • Business Storytelling & Executive Presentations
Pillar 05

Real-World Capstone Project

Put all 4 skills to the test in a comprehensive capstone project simulating corporate business scenarios. Extract with SQL, clean and analyze with Python, visualize in Tableau, and receive 1-on-1 mentor feedback.

  • Real Business Dataset Problem Solving
  • End-to-End Pipeline & Analytical Documentation
  • Live Presentation & Technical Evaluation
  • Verified NextGen Certificate of Completion
